**Notes on Integrals and Area**

**1. Definite Integral Calculation:**

*   **Problem:** $\int_{-3}^{3} (x+1) dx$
*   **Antiderivative:** $= [\frac{x^2}{2} + x + C]_{-3}^{3}$
*   **Evaluation:** $= (\frac{3^2}{2} + 3) - (\frac{(-3)^2}{2} + (-3))$
    $= (\frac{9}{2} + 3) - (\frac{9}{2} - 3)$
    $= (4.5 + 3) - (4.5 - 3)$
    $= 7.5 - 1.5$
    $= 6$

**2. Geometric Interpretation (Net Signed Area):**

*   The function is $f(x)=x+1$.
*   **Area 1 (A₁):** Region above the x-axis, from $x=-1$ to $x=3$.
    *   $A_1 = 8$
*   **Area 2 (A₂):** Region below the x-axis, from $x=-3$ to $x=-1$.
    *   $A_2 = -2$
*   **Integral Value (Net Signed Area):** "The area of the 3rd quartile will be negative, therefore my Integral will be $A_1 + A_2 = 8 - 2 = 6$."

**3. Total Area Calculation:**

*   "However, I could be asked to calculate the area (not the integral). Negative areas must be turned to positive."
*   **Total Area:** $|A_1| + |A_2| = |8| + |-2| = 8 + 2 = 10$

**4. Integral Properties for Area Calculation:**

*   "Remember integrals are sums, hence:"
    $\int_{-3}^{3} (x+1) dx = \int_{-3}^{-1} (x+1) dx + \int_{-1}^{3} (x+1) dx$

*   "If I were to calculate the area, I could divide the integral into a sum, and divide it exactly where it becomes negative, where $f(x)=0$."
*   "Now, the negative area is turned to positive to find the area with absolute value."
    $\int_{-3}^{3} (x+1) dx = |\int_{-3}^{-1} (x+1) dx| + \int_{-1}^{3} (x+1) dx$

**5. Property for Reversing Integration Limits:**

*   "The negative Area can also be turned Positive by using the last integral property:"
    $\int_{a}^{b} f(x) dx = - \int_{b}^{a} f(x) dx$
*   **Example:** $\int_{-4}^{-2} f(x) dx = - \int_{-2}^{-4} f(x) dx$
