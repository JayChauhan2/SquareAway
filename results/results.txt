## Unit 7 Lesson 1: Algebraic Expressions

### Vocabulary

*   **Algebraic Expression:** An expression that may have **numbers**, **operations**, and one or more **variables**.
*   **Variable:** A **symbol** that represents one or more **numbers**.
*   **Term:** Each **number**, **variable**, or groups of **numbers** and **variables**, separated by an **operation sign** (+, -, x).
*   **Coefficient:** The **numerical** factor of a term that contains a **variable**.
*   **Constant:** A term **without** a **variable**.

### Steps

1.  Write the expression as a **SUM**.
2.  Identify the terms, coefficients, and constants.

### Example

**Expression:** `2z² - 5y + 3`

1.  **Write as a sum:** `2z² + (-5y) + 3`
    *   This is an **expression as a sum**.

2.  **Identify components:**
    *   **TERMS:** `2z²`, `-5y`, `3`
    *   **COEFFICIENTS:** `2`, `-5`
    *   **CONSTANTS:** `3`
