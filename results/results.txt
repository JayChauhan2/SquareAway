# MATH STUFF

## Undefined
*   # / 0 (anything w/ "0" in denominator)

## Speed
*   Speed = Distance / Time
    *   (think of running & figuring out the pace)

## Exponential Form
*   y = a(b)^x
    *   `a` = y-intercept
    *   `b` = rate
    *   b < 1: decay (graph shown)
    *   b > 1: growth (graph shown)

## Quadratic
*   c / a = product of roots / zeroes
*   -b / a = sum of roots or zeroes
*   Cap (referring to vertex/turning point):
    *   -b / 2a = find Axis of Symmetry (AoS) (min-max- point on parabola) x-coordinate

## Discriminant (Find # of real solutions)
*   b^2 - 4ac = 0: 1 Real solution
*   b^2 - 4ac < 0: no real solution
*   b^2 - 4ac > 0: 2 real solutions

## Percent Change
*   (New - Old) / Old x 100%

## Systems of Equations
*   **No Solution (Parallel):**
    *   SAME slope
    *   different y-intercept
*   **Infinitely Many Solutions:**
    *   SAME EQUATION!
    *   (meaning same slope, same y-intercept)
