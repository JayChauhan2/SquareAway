**Estimating Area between Curves**
Unit 8
Date: Nov 3, 2022

We can calculate this area by estimating with rectangles and taking the limit to get the definite Integral.

**Example: Area between y = 2-x and y = x²**
*   **Height:** `(2 - x) - x²`
*   **Δx:** `(1.5 - (-2)) / n = 3.5 / n`
*   **Riemann Sum:** `Σ ( (2 - xᵢ₋₁) - xᵢ₋₁² ) Δx` (from i=1 to n)
*   **Definite Integral:** `∫(-2 to 1.5) [ (2 - x) - x² ] dx`

---
**Negative Integral Values**
For a function `F(t)`, the definite integral `∫(a to b) F(t) dt` represents the net signed area.
If A, C, E represent positive areas (above the x-axis) and B, D represent negative areas (below the x-axis), then:
`∫(a to b) F(t) dt = A + C + E - B - D`

---
**Midpoint Rule**

*   Left[n] and Right[n] (Riemann sums using left and right endpoints) for approximating definite Integrals are inefficient and crude estimates.
*   To use a more accurate and efficient method is the Midpoint Rule.

**Comparison of Approximation Methods:**
1.  **Left Riemann Sum:** (Rectangles use the left endpoint of each subinterval for height)
2.  **Right Riemann Sum:** (Rectangles use the right endpoint of each subinterval for height)
3.  **Midpoint Rule:** (Rectangles use the midpoint of each subinterval for height)
    *   Cancels out errors and is more accurate.
    *   For linear functions, the Midpoint Rule gives a value exactly equal to the integral.
