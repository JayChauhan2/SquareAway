**Estimating Area Between Curves**

We can calculate this area by estimating with rectangles and taking the limit to get the definite Integral.

[Graph showing y=x^2 and y=2-x, with shaded area between them]

*   Height = (2 - x) - x^2
*   ΔX = [1.5 - (-2)] / n = 3.5 / n

∑ |Height| |width| = ∑ (from i=1 to n) [ (2 - X_(i-1)) - X_(i-1)^2 ] ΔX

∫ (from -2 to 1.5) [ (2 - X) - X^2 ] dx

**Midpoint Rule**

*   Left[In] and Right[In] for approximating definite Integrals are inefficient and crude estimates.
*   To use a more accurate and efficient [method] is the Midpoint Rule.

[Three graphs illustrating Left Riemann Sum, Right Riemann Sum, and Midpoint Rule approximations]

*   For linear functions -> Midpoint = ∫ (from a to b) F(x)dx
*   Errors cancel out and [it is] more accurate.
*   Value exactly equal to integral [for linear functions].
