### Special Triangles
(Often given in the test, but good to know mentally)

1.  **30-60-90 Triangle**
    *   Sides are in the ratio: `x`, `x√3`, `2x`
    *   (Side opposite 30° is x, opposite 60° is x√3, hypotenuse opposite 90° is 2x)

2.  **45-45-90 Triangle**
    *   Sides are in the ratio: `x`, `x`, `x√2`
    *   (The two legs are x, the hypotenuse is x√2)
