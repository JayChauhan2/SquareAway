### Estimating Area between curves

We can calculate this area by estimating with rectangles and taking the limit to get the definite Integral.

**Height** = $(2-x)-x^2$
$\Delta X = \frac{[1.5 - (-2)]}{n} = \frac{3.5}{n}$

$\sum |Height| \text{ } |width| = \sum_{i=1}^{n} |(2-X_{i-1})-X_{i-1}^2| \Delta X$
$\int_{-2}^{1.5} [(2-x)-x^2] dx$

### Midpoint Rule

*   Left[n] and Right[n] for approximating definite Integrals are inefficient and crude estimates.
*   To use a more accurate and efficient is the **Midpoint Rule**

*(Accompanying graphs illustrate approximations with rectangles)*

**Left**
*(Rectangles use the left endpoint of each interval to determine height)*

**Right**
*(Rectangles use the right endpoint of each interval to determine height)*

**Mid**
*(Rectangles use the midpoint of each interval to determine height)*

*   linear functions $\to$ Midpoint $= \int_a^b f(x)dx$
    *   Cancels out and more accurate
    *   Value exactly equal to integral
